The Art of Becoming

Kelsie E. Stark is a medically retired Army Veteran, fine-art photographer, and poet based in El Paso, Texas.

Her work explores the intersection of trauma and liberation, using visual and written narratives to document the strength found in vulnerability. As a Resident Creative for Strong Bonds Resilience, she captures the "industrial soul" of historic recovery.
